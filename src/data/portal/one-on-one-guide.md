# TRNTY Manager 1:1 Template

Public-safe template for a manager 1:1. Short, repeatable, same shape every week. The point is one commitment, one proof check, one homework assignment.

This page does not include pay, bonuses, revenue minimums, door counts, per-door rates, housing, contract language, or MD details. Those live in the manager system doc, not here.

---

## What this meeting is

A short coaching meeting, not a report. Pull whatever you need before you get on the call so the time is spent on the person in front of you.

Numbers first in the private manager prep, if you use them. Then one skill. Then one commitment. The Tuesday team meeting and Wednesday leadership development stay on the calendar. They do not replace a 1:1.

---

## Cadence

- In season: every direct, every week. Keep it tight, about 20 to 30 minutes.
- Rookies, anyone in their first two weeks in market, and anyone who is slipping: weekly, no exceptions.
- Vets who are at standard, locked for next season, and current on training: biweekly is fine in the offseason. Move them back to weekly the moment activity, quality, or mindset slips.
- Set the first 1:1 on the offer call, before they start the training course.
- Book the next 1:1 before you hang up.

---

## Agenda, 25 minutes

| Time | Block | What happens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0-3 | Check-in and mindset | Energy, sleep, what is in their head. If they want to quit, play the sunblock video they recorded. |
| 3-10 | Last stretch | What got done, quality, training. Stay on one issue, not a tour of every metric. Use the measures that belong to that vertical. Do not write door counts, pay, or revenue on the public form. |
| 10-13 | Last week's commitment | Did they do it. Yes or no, plus proof. No stories on a miss. |
| 13-22 | Skill drill | One skill only. Door approach, objection handling, or close. Rep runs it. Manager pauses and corrects. Rep runs it again. |
| 22-25 | This week's commitment | One sentence. Pick the focus: Mind (skill), Heart (desire), or Feet (activity). Name the drill and when they will practice it. Book the next 1:1. |

Pest drill road map, when the skill block is a pitch rep: Intro, Bridge, Price Sheet, Objections, Close. Memorize the skeleton before they start riffing. Objection formula: Yes, Address, Close, then stop talking. Soft close first, to surface the real objection. Hard close only when they are ready to buy. The environmental, or backyard, close is the one to master.

## Same-night text to the rep

Send it before the night is over. Keep it short, exactly in this shape:

Commitment is [X]. Drill is [Y], run it [when].

Sent: yes / no    Time sent: _______________

That text is the commitment you will check, with proof, at the next 1:1.

## After the meeting

1. Fill the template during the 1:1.
2. Log it the same day.
3. Text the rep the one commitment and one drill, in the line above, before the night is over.
4. Scorecards, if you keep them, stay with the manager. The rep does not get written scores.
