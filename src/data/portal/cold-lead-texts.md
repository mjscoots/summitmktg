# Cold lead texts (rookie and vet)

DRAFT. Manager-only. These are drafts in Mathew's voice. Nothing goes out in his name without his OK on the exact text. No pay numbers, deal terms, housing, or market promises in any text. Pricing happens on a call.

Rules from the kit:

- No link in any first text or no-reply follow-up. The booking link goes out only once they reply.
- Every recruiting call books at `calendly.com/mathewjoyce/interviews`.
- Do not invent a [page] or a [source]. Use them only if they are real.
- "Roster closes once the seats fill" is the standing line. No firm close date is used.
- They say no, or stop: no more texts. Mark them Cold or do-not-contact.

## ROOKIE

### First text (no link)

Option 1. Use only when you have a real page, account, or mutual follow. Do not invent one.

```
Hey, saw we both follow [page] so I figured I'd hit you up.
I manage a sales team and we're adding a few people for the summer.

You already locked down a summer internship or still open to hearing options?
```

Option 2. Use when there is no shared page or mutual follow.

```
Hey [name], this is Mathew. I manage a sales team and we're adding a few people for the summer.
You open to hearing about it?
```

Option 3. Shorter cut for mixing a batch.

```
Yo [name], what's good. This is Mathew. If a seat this summer makes sense, let me know and I'll send you a time
```

If a number was passed by a rep or another manager, and there was no prior conversation with Mathew:

```
Hey [name], this is Mathew. [source] passed me your number so I figured I'd hit you up.
You open to hearing about a seat this summer?
```

### Once they reply

Positive reply (yes, interested, tell me more):

```
Bet. Grab 30 min here, first call is just getting to know you
calendly.com/mathewjoyce/interviews
```

They say they already sold a season: switch to the vet track. Ask the vet question and send calendly.com/mathewjoyce/interviews only if they confirm.

They ask about pay, housing, or a market:

```
That one we go over on the call. Grab 30 min here
calendly.com/mathewjoyce/interviews
```

They say no, or stop: no more texts. Mark the row Cold or do-not-contact.

### Follow-up sequence (no reply yet, no link)

24 hours, no reply

```
Yo [name], what's good, just checking in! Anything I can help you with?
```

72 hours, no reply

```
Hey [name], how you doing? Anything I could help you with?
Roster closes once the seats fill, so if you want in just reply and I'll send a time
```

Last touch (about 7 days after the first text, then mark Cold if there's no reply)

```
Hey [name], how you doing? If a seat this summer still makes sense, reply and I'll send a time over
If not, no worries
```

If they reply to any follow-up, send the "Once they reply" link text.

```

calendly.com/mathewjoyce/interviews
```
