## Welcome to Summit Trinity

you're in. here's what happens next and what we expect. read the whole thing.

## Step 1: Paper (week of your offer)

1. Sign your agreement. It comes through Hawx Nidus (email from Hawx). Read the 3-week commitment and housing sections; that's exactly what we agreed to.
2. Background check through Hawx when it's sent.
3. You get added to GETHawx. Check that your phone and email are right.
4. Join the team group chat. Respond to chat when you can.

## Step 2: Your first 1:1 (set on your offer call)

- Your manager books it before you start the training course.
- Bring: what you want out of the summer, and what would stop you.
- Record your "sunblock" video: a short video to your future self on why you're doing this. We play it back on the hard days.
- Name your Board of Mentors (2 to 3 people who keep you in the game).

## Step 3: Training (now until you land in market)

- Training course + the pitch, sent after your agreement.
- Wednesday trainings, 7 PM EST. Mandatory.
- Learn the road map: Intro, Bridge, Price Sheet, Objections, Close. Memorize the skeleton before you riff.
- Objection formula: Yes, Address, Close, then stop talking.

## Step 4: Your day in market

- Season runs May 1 to Aug 31.
- Meeting 9:30 to 10:30 AM. Knock noon to dark. Saturdays 10:30 to 5:30. Sundays off.
- Log every sale in GETHawx the same day.

## Step 5: Every week

- 1:1 with your manager, 20 to 30 minutes: last week's commitment, one skill drill, this week's commitment.
- Tuesday team meeting. Wednesday leadership development.

## Culture

- Negativity in the group chat or talking sideways about another rep is a Constitution violation. One warning, then removal.
- Want to quit? Tell your manager first. We play your sunblock video, call your Board of Mentors, and talk that night.
